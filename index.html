Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

SwatPlan does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Your schedule, tasks, and preferences stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

SwatPlan stores the information you enter or select so the app can remember your setup between launches. This may include:

  • Your selected courses and schedule
  • Your tasks, due dates, and scheduled work times
  • Your display, ranking, and reminder preferences
  • Your blocked-time preferences

This information is stored locally on your device. SwatPlan does not operate an account system or upload this information to a developer-controlled server.

Notifications

If you enable class reminders, SwatPlan asks iOS to schedule notifications on your device. These are local notifications; SwatPlan does not use a remote push notification service. You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

External links

SwatPlan includes optional links to external services, including Google Maps, Swarthmore College course information, and Rate My Professors. When you choose one of these links, the external service may process information under its own privacy policy. SwatPlan does not receive data about your activity on those services.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

SwatPlan does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you remove it using the app’s controls or uninstall the app. You can disable pending class reminders in the app or in iOS Settings.

Children’s privacy

SwatPlan is intended for college students and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if SwatPlan’s features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the SwatPlan public repository.

Independent project

SwatPlan is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Swarthmore College, Google, or Rate My Professors.
